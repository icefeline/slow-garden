# slow garden: tone listening samples

12 real outputs from `lib/utils/insight-generator-v2.ts` (same code the app runs), one fixed
voice-test set. Generated 2026-10-06.

## how to use

1. In ElevenLabs, open Text to Speech and pick ONE voice. Use the same voice for every clip,
   so you are judging the writing and not the voice.
2. Paste the **paste this** block for each sample (key phrase, then insight). The app only
   voices `insight` by default; the key phrase is included so you can hear whether it sits
   well in front of it.
3. Under each, fill in the notes: quote the exact line that bothered you, and write what you
   would say instead. A rewrite from you is the most useful thing you can send back.
4. Optional second pass: paste the **action** text on its own.

Mark each: ✅ sounds like us · 🟡 close, one thing off · ❌ doesn't sound like us

## things to listen for across all 12

- Does it sound like one person, or like several templates stitched together?
- The planet sentence ("Saturn on your Moon in home - ...") is the same shape every time. Does the repetition show up when heard aloud?
- Some key phrases repeat ("hope based on truth" appears 4 times, "what needed to break" 3 times). Does it land as a motif or as a glitch?
- Several actions start with an instruction ("stop explaining…", "do that much today…"). The tone rules say the action is a noticing practice, not a to-do. Does it sound like that?
